1:1 Blessed is the man who doesn’t walk in the counsel of the wicked,
1:1 nor stand on the path of sinners,
1:1 nor sit in the seat of scoffers;
1:2 but his delight is in Yahweh’s law.
1:2 On his law he meditates day and night.
1:3 He will be like a tree planted by the streams of water,
1:3 that produces its fruit in its season,
1:3 whose leaf also does not wither.
1:3 Whatever he does shall prosper.
1:4 The wicked are not so,
1:4 but are like the chaff which the wind drives away.
1:5 Therefore the wicked shall not stand in the judgment,
1:5 nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.
1:6 For Yahweh knows the way of the righteous,
1:6 but the way of the wicked shall perish.
2:1 Why do the nations rage,
2:1 and the peoples plot a vain thing?
2:2 The kings of the earth take a stand,
2:2 and the rulers take counsel together,
2:2 against Yahweh, and against his Anointed, saying,
2:3 “Let’s break their bonds apart,
2:3 and cast their cords from us.”
2:4 He who sits in the heavens will laugh.
2:4 The Lord will have them in derision.
2:5 Then he will speak to them in his anger,
2:5 and terrify them in his wrath:
2:6 “Yet I have set my King on my holy hill of Zion.”
2:7 I will tell of the decree:
2:7 Yahweh said to me, “You are my son.
2:7 Today I have become your father.
2:8 Ask of me, and I will give the nations for your inheritance,
2:8 the uttermost parts of the earth for your possession.
2:9 You shall break them with a rod of iron.
2:9 You shall dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.”
2:10 Now therefore be wise, you kings.
2:10 Be instructed, you judges of the earth.
2:11 Serve Yahweh with fear,
2:11 and rejoice with trembling.
2:12 Give sincere homage to the Son, lest he be angry, and you perish on the way,
2:12 for his wrath will soon be kindled.
2:12 Blessed are all those who take refuge in him.
