1:1 The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel:
1:2 to know wisdom and instruction;
1:2 to discern the words of understanding;
1:3 to receive instruction in wise dealing,
1:3 in righteousness, justice, and equity;
1:4 to give prudence to the simple,
1:4 knowledge and discretion to the young man:
1:5 that the wise man may hear, and increase in learning;
1:5 that the man of understanding may attain to sound counsel:
1:6 to understand a proverb, and parables,
1:6 the words and riddles of the wise.
1:7 The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of knowledge;
1:7 but the foolish despise wisdom and instruction.
1:8 My son, listen to your father’s instruction,
1:8 and don’t forsake your mother’s teaching:
1:9 for they will be a garland to grace your head,
1:9 and chains around your neck.
1:10 My son, if sinners entice you,
1:10 don’t consent.
1:11 If they say, “Come with us.
1:11 Let’s lay in wait for blood.
1:11 Let’s lurk secretly for the innocent without cause.
1:12 Let’s swallow them up alive like Sheol,
1:12 and whole, like those who go down into the pit.
1:13 We’ll find all valuable wealth.
1:13 We’ll fill our houses with plunder.
1:14 You shall cast your lot among us.
1:14 We’ll all have one purse.”
1:15 My son, don’t walk on the path with them.
1:15 Keep your foot from their path,
1:16 for their feet run to evil.
1:16 They hurry to shed blood.
1:17 For the net is spread in vain in the sight of any bird;
1:18 but these lay in wait for their own blood.
1:18 They lurk secretly for their own lives.
1:19 So are the ways of everyone who is greedy for gain.
1:19 It takes away the life of its owners.
1:20 Wisdom calls aloud in the street.
1:20 She utters her voice in the public squares.
1:21 She calls at the head of noisy places.
1:21 At the entrance of the city gates, she utters her words:
1:22 “How long, you simple ones, will you love simplicity?
1:22 How long will mockers delight themselves in mockery,
1:22 and fools hate knowledge?
1:23 Turn at my reproof.
1:23 Behold, I will pour out my spirit on you.
1:23 I will make known my words to you.
1:24 Because I have called, and you have refused;
1:24 I have stretched out my hand, and no one has paid attention;
1:25 but you have ignored all my counsel,
1:25 and wanted none of my reproof;
1:26 I also will laugh at your disaster.
1:26 I will mock when calamity overtakes you,
1:27 when calamity overtakes you like a storm,
1:27 when your disaster comes on like a whirlwind,
1:27 when distress and anguish come on you.
1:28 Then they will call on me, but I will not answer.
1:28 They will seek me diligently, but they will not find me;
1:29 because they hated knowledge,
1:29 and didn’t choose the fear of Yahweh.
1:30 They wanted none of my counsel.
1:30 They despised all my reproof.
1:31 Therefore they will eat of the fruit of their own way,
1:31 and be filled with their own schemes.
1:32 For the backsliding of the simple will kill them.
1:32 The careless ease of fools will destroy them.
1:33 But whoever listens to me will dwell securely,
1:33 and will be at ease, without fear of harm.”
2:1 My son, if you will receive my words,
2:1 and store up my commandments within you,
2:2 so as to turn your ear to wisdom,
2:2 and apply your heart to understanding;
2:3 yes, if you call out for discernment,
2:3 and lift up your voice for understanding;
2:4 if you seek her as silver,
2:4 and search for her as for hidden treasures:
2:5 then you will understand the fear of Yahweh,
2:5 and find the knowledge of God.
2:6 For Yahweh gives wisdom.
2:6 Out of his mouth comes knowledge and understanding.
2:7 He lays up sound wisdom for the upright.
2:7 He is a shield to those who walk in integrity,
2:8 that he may guard the paths of justice,
2:8 and preserve the way of his saints.
2:9 Then you will understand righteousness and justice,
2:9 equity and every good path.
2:10 For wisdom will enter into your heart.
2:10 Knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.
2:11 Discretion will watch over you.
2:11 Understanding will keep you,
2:12 to deliver you from the way of evil,
2:12 from the men who speak perverse things,
2:13 who forsake the paths of uprightness,
2:13 to walk in the ways of darkness,
2:14 who rejoice to do evil,
2:14 and delight in the perverseness of evil,
2:15 who are crooked in their ways,
2:15 and wayward in their paths,
2:16 to deliver you from the strange woman,
2:16 even from the foreigner who flatters with her words,
2:17 who forsakes the friend of her youth,
2:17 and forgets the covenant of her God;
2:18 for her house leads down to death,
2:18 her paths to the departed spirits.
2:19 None who go to her return again,
2:19 neither do they attain to the paths of life.
2:20 So you may walk in the way of good men,
2:20 and keep the paths of the righteous.
2:21 For the upright will dwell in the land.
2:21 The perfect will remain in it.
2:22 But the wicked will be cut off from the land.
2:22 The treacherous will be rooted out of it.
3:1 My son, don’t forget my teaching;
3:1 but let your heart keep my commandments:
3:2 for they will add to you length of days,
3:2 years of life, and peace.
3:3 Don’t let kindness and truth forsake you.
3:3 Bind them around your neck.
3:3 Write them on the tablet of your heart.
3:4 So you will find favor,
3:4 and good understanding in the sight of God and man.
3:5 Trust in Yahweh with all your heart,
3:5 and don’t lean on your own understanding.
3:6 In all your ways acknowledge him,
3:6 and he will make your paths straight.
3:7 Don’t be wise in your own eyes.
3:7 Fear Yahweh, and depart from evil.
3:8 It will be health to your body,
3:8 and nourishment to your bones.
3:9 Honor Yahweh with your substance,
3:9 with the first fruits of all your increase:
3:10 so your barns will be filled with plenty,
3:10 and your vats will overflow with new wine.
3:11 My son, don’t despise Yahweh’s discipline,
3:11 neither be weary of his correction;
3:12 for whom Yahweh loves, he corrects,
3:12 even as a father reproves the son in whom he delights.
3:13 Happy is the man who finds wisdom,
3:13 the man who gets understanding.
3:14 For her good profit is better than getting silver,
3:14 and her return is better than fine gold.
3:15 She is more precious than rubies.
3:15 None of the things you can desire are to be compared to her.
3:16 Length of days is in her right hand.
3:16 In her left hand are riches and honor.
3:17 Her ways are ways of pleasantness.
3:17 All her paths are peace.
3:18 She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her.
3:18 Happy is everyone who retains her.
3:19 By wisdom Yahweh founded the earth.
3:19 By understanding, he established the heavens.
3:20 By his knowledge, the depths were broken up,
3:20 and the skies drop down the dew.
3:21 My son, let them not depart from your eyes.
3:21 Keep sound wisdom and discretion:
3:22 so they will be life to your soul,
3:22 and grace for your neck.
3:23 Then you shall walk in your way securely.
3:23 Your foot won’t stumble.
3:24 When you lie down, you will not be afraid.
3:24 Yes, you will lie down, and your sleep will be sweet.
3:25 Don’t be afraid of sudden fear,
3:25 neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it comes;
3:26 for Yahweh will be your confidence,
3:26 and will keep your foot from being taken.
3:27 Don’t withhold good from those to whom it is due,
3:27 when it is in the power of your hand to do it.
3:28 Don’t say to your neighbor, “Go, and come again;
3:28 tomorrow I will give it to you,”
3:28 when you have it by you.
3:29 Don’t devise evil against your neighbor,
3:29 since he dwells securely by you.
3:30 Don’t strive with a man without cause,
3:30 if he has done you no harm.
3:31 Don’t envy the man of violence.
3:31 Choose none of his ways.
3:32 For the perverse is an abomination to Yahweh,
3:32 but his friendship is with the upright.
3:33 Yahweh’s curse is in the house of the wicked,
3:33 but he blesses the habitation of the righteous.
3:34 Surely he mocks the mockers,
3:34 but he gives grace to the humble.
3:35 The wise will inherit glory,
3:35 but shame will be the promotion of fools.
4:1 Listen, sons, to a father’s instruction.
4:1 Pay attention and know understanding;
4:2 for I give you sound learning.
4:2 Don’t forsake my law.
4:3 For I was a son to my father,
4:3 tender and an only child in the sight of my mother.
4:4 He taught me, and said to me:
4:4 “Let your heart retain my words.
4:4 Keep my commandments, and live.
4:5 Get wisdom.
4:5 Get understanding.
4:5 Don’t forget, and don’t deviate from the words of my mouth.
4:6 Don’t forsake her, and she will preserve you.
4:6 Love her, and she will keep you.
4:7 Wisdom is supreme.
4:7 Get wisdom.
4:7 Yes, though it costs all your possessions, get understanding.
4:8 Esteem her, and she will exalt you.
4:8 She will bring you to honor when you embrace her.
4:9 She will give to your head a garland of grace.
4:9 She will deliver a crown of splendor to you.”
4:10 Listen, my son, and receive my sayings.
4:10 The years of your life will be many.
4:11 I have taught you in the way of wisdom.
4:11 I have led you in straight paths.
4:12 When you go, your steps will not be hampered.
4:12 When you run, you will not stumble.
4:13 Take firm hold of instruction.
4:13 Don’t let her go.
4:13 Keep her, for she is your life.
4:14 Don’t enter into the path of the wicked.
4:14 Don’t walk in the way of evil men.
4:15 Avoid it, and don’t pass by it.
4:15 Turn from it, and pass on.
4:16 For they don’t sleep unless they do evil.
4:16 Their sleep is taken away, unless they make someone fall.
4:17 For they eat the bread of wickedness
4:17 and drink the wine of violence.
4:18 But the path of the righteous is like the dawning light
4:18 that shines more and more until the perfect day.
4:19 The way of the wicked is like darkness.
4:19 They don’t know what they stumble over.
4:20 My son, attend to my words.
4:20 Turn your ear to my sayings.
4:21 Let them not depart from your eyes.
4:21 Keep them in the center of your heart.
4:22 For they are life to those who find them,
4:22 and health to their whole body.
4:23 Keep your heart with all diligence,
4:23 for out of it is the wellspring of life.
4:24 Put away from yourself a perverse mouth.
4:24 Put corrupt lips far from you.
4:25 Let your eyes look straight ahead.
4:25 Fix your gaze directly before you.
4:26 Make the path of your feet level.
4:26 Let all of your ways be established.
4:27 Don’t turn to the right hand nor to the left.
4:27 Remove your foot from evil.
5:1 My son, pay attention to my wisdom.
5:1 Turn your ear to my understanding,
5:2 that you may maintain discretion,
5:2 that your lips may preserve knowledge.
5:3 For the lips of an adulteress drip honey.
5:3 Her mouth is smoother than oil,
5:4 but in the end she is as bitter as wormwood,
5:4 and as sharp as a two-edged sword.
5:5 Her feet go down to death.
5:5 Her steps lead straight to Sheol.
5:6 She gives no thought to the way of life.
5:6 Her ways are crooked, and she doesn’t know it.
5:7 Now therefore, my sons, listen to me.
5:7 Don’t depart from the words of my mouth.
5:8 Remove your way far from her.
5:8 Don’t come near the door of her house,
5:9 lest you give your honor to others,
5:9 and your years to the cruel one;
5:10 lest strangers feast on your wealth,
5:10 and your labors enrich another man’s house.
5:11 You will groan at your latter end,
5:11 when your flesh and your body are consumed,
5:12 and say, “How I have hated instruction,
5:12 and my heart despised reproof;
5:13 neither have I obeyed the voice of my teachers,
5:13 nor turned my ear to those who instructed me!
5:14 I have come to the brink of utter ruin,
5:14 among the gathered assembly.”
5:15 Drink water out of your own cistern,
5:15 running water out of your own well.
5:16 Should your springs overflow in the streets,
5:16 streams of water in the public squares?
5:17 Let them be for yourself alone,
5:17 not for strangers with you.
5:18 Let your spring be blessed.
5:18 Rejoice in the wife of your youth.
5:19 A loving doe and a graceful deer—
5:19 let her breasts satisfy you at all times.
5:19 Be captivated always with her love.
5:20 For why should you, my son, be captivated with an adulteress?
5:20 Why embrace the bosom of another?
5:21 For the ways of man are before Yahweh’s eyes.
5:21 He examines all his paths.
5:22 The evil deeds of the wicked ensnare him.
5:22 The cords of his sin hold him firmly.
5:23 He will die for lack of instruction.
5:23 In the greatness of his folly, he will go astray.
6:1 My son, if you have become collateral for your neighbor,
6:1 if you have struck your hands in pledge for a stranger,
6:2 you are trapped by the words of your mouth;
6:2 you are ensnared with the words of your mouth.
6:3 Do this now, my son, and deliver yourself,
6:3 since you have come into the hand of your neighbor.
6:3 Go, humble yourself.
6:3 Press your plea with your neighbor.
6:4 Give no sleep to your eyes,
6:4 nor slumber to your eyelids.
6:5 Free yourself, like a gazelle from the hand of the hunter,
6:5 like a bird from the snare of the fowler.
6:6 Go to the ant, you sluggard.
6:6 Consider her ways, and be wise;
6:7 which having no chief, overseer, or ruler,
6:8 provides her bread in the summer,
6:8 and gathers her food in the harvest.
6:9 How long will you sleep, sluggard?
6:9 When will you arise out of your sleep?
6:10 A little sleep, a little slumber,
6:10 a little folding of the hands to sleep:
6:11 so your poverty will come as a robber,
6:11 and your scarcity as an armed man.
6:12 A worthless person, a man of iniquity,
6:12 is he who walks with a perverse mouth,
6:13 who winks with his eyes, who signals with his feet,
6:13 who motions with his fingers,
6:14 in whose heart is perverseness,
6:14 who devises evil continually,
6:14 who always sows discord.
6:15 Therefore his calamity will come suddenly.
6:15 He will be broken suddenly, and that without remedy.
6:16 There are six things which Yahweh hates;
6:16 yes, seven which are an abomination to him:
6:17 arrogant eyes, a lying tongue,
6:17 hands that shed innocent blood,
6:18 a heart that devises wicked schemes,
6:18 feet that are swift in running to mischief,
6:19 a false witness who utters lies,
6:19 and he who sows discord among brothers.
6:20 My son, keep your father’s commandment,
6:20 and don’t forsake your mother’s teaching.
6:21 Bind them continually on your heart.
6:21 Tie them around your neck.
6:22 When you walk, it will lead you.
6:22 When you sleep, it will watch over you.
6:22 When you awake, it will talk with you.
6:23 For the commandment is a lamp,
6:23 and the law is light.
6:23 Reproofs of instruction are the way of life,
6:24 to keep you from the immoral woman,
6:24 from the flattery of the wayward wife’s tongue.
6:25 Don’t lust after her beauty in your heart,
6:25 neither let her captivate you with her eyelids.
6:26 For a prostitute reduces you to a piece of bread.
6:26 The adulteress hunts for your precious life.
6:27 Can a man scoop fire into his lap,
6:27 and his clothes not be burned?
6:28 Or can one walk on hot coals,
6:28 and his feet not be scorched?
6:29 So is he who goes in to his neighbor’s wife.
6:29 Whoever touches her will not be unpunished.
6:30 Men don’t despise a thief
6:30 if he steals to satisfy himself when he is hungry;
6:31 but if he is found, he shall restore seven times.
6:31 He shall give all the wealth of his house.
6:32 He who commits adultery with a woman is void of understanding.
6:32 He who does it destroys his own soul.
6:33 He will get wounds and dishonor.
6:33 His reproach will not be wiped away.
6:34 For jealousy arouses the fury of the husband.
6:34 He won’t spare in the day of vengeance.
6:35 He won’t regard any ransom,
6:35 neither will he rest content, though you give many gifts.
7:1 My son, keep my words.
7:1 Lay up my commandments within you.
7:2 Keep my commandments and live!
7:2 Guard my teaching as the apple of your eye.
7:3 Bind them on your fingers.
7:3 Write them on the tablet of your heart.
7:4 Tell wisdom, “You are my sister.”
7:4 Call understanding your relative,
7:5 that they may keep you from the strange woman,
7:5 from the foreigner who flatters with her words.
7:6 For at the window of my house,
7:6 I looked out through my lattice.
7:7 I saw among the simple ones.
7:7 I discerned among the youths a young man void of understanding,
7:8 passing through the street near her corner,
7:8 he went the way to her house,
7:9 in the twilight, in the evening of the day,
7:9 in the middle of the night and in the darkness.
7:10 Behold, there a woman met him with the attire of a prostitute,
7:10 and with crafty intent.
7:11 She is loud and defiant.
7:11 Her feet don’t stay in her house.
7:12 Now she is in the streets, now in the squares,
7:12 and lurking at every corner.
7:13 So she caught him, and kissed him.
7:13 With an impudent face she said to him:
7:14 “Sacrifices of peace offerings are with me.
7:14 Today I have paid my vows.
7:15 Therefore I came out to meet you,
7:15 to diligently seek your face,
7:15 and I have found you.
7:16 I have spread my couch with carpets of tapestry,
7:16 with striped cloths of the yarn of Egypt.
7:17 I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.
7:18 Come, let’s take our fill of loving until the morning.
7:18 Let’s solace ourselves with loving.
7:19 For my husband isn’t at home.
7:19 He has gone on a long journey.
7:20 He has taken a bag of money with him.
7:20 He will come home at the full moon.”
7:21 With persuasive words, she led him astray.
7:21 With the flattering of her lips, she seduced him.
7:22 He followed her immediately,
7:22 as an ox goes to the slaughter,
7:22 as a fool stepping into a noose.
7:23 Until an arrow strikes through his liver,
7:23 as a bird hurries to the snare,
7:23 and doesn’t know that it will cost his life.
7:24 Now therefore, sons, listen to me.
7:24 Pay attention to the words of my mouth.
7:25 Don’t let your heart turn to her ways.
7:25 Don’t go astray in her paths,
7:26 for she has thrown down many wounded.
7:26 Yes, all her slain are a mighty army.
7:27 Her house is the way to Sheol,
7:27 going down to the rooms of death.
8:1 Doesn’t wisdom cry out?
8:1 Doesn’t understanding raise her voice?
8:2 On the top of high places by the way,
8:2 where the paths meet, she stands.
8:3 Beside the gates, at the entry of the city,
8:3 at the entry doors, she cries aloud:
8:4 “I call to you men!
8:4 I send my voice to the sons of mankind.
8:5 You simple, understand prudence!
8:5 You fools, be of an understanding heart!
8:6 Hear, for I will speak excellent things.
8:6 The opening of my lips is for right things.
8:7 For my mouth speaks truth.
8:7 Wickedness is an abomination to my lips.
8:8 All the words of my mouth are in righteousness.
8:8 There is nothing crooked or perverse in them.
8:9 They are all plain to him who understands,
8:9 right to those who find knowledge.
8:10 Receive my instruction rather than silver,
8:10 knowledge rather than choice gold.
8:11 For wisdom is better than rubies.
8:11 All the things that may be desired can’t be compared to it.
8:12 “I, wisdom, have made prudence my dwelling.
8:12 Find out knowledge and discretion.
8:13 The fear of Yahweh is to hate evil.
8:13 I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way, and the perverse mouth.
8:14 Counsel and sound knowledge are mine.
8:14 I have understanding and power.
8:15 By me kings reign,
8:15 and princes decree justice.
8:16 By me princes rule,
8:16 nobles, and all the righteous rulers of the earth.
8:17 I love those who love me.
8:17 Those who seek me diligently will find me.
8:18 With me are riches, honor,
8:18 enduring wealth, and prosperity.
8:19 My fruit is better than gold, yes, than fine gold,
8:19 my yield than choice silver.
8:20 I walk in the way of righteousness,
8:20 in the middle of the paths of justice,
8:21 that I may give wealth to those who love me.
8:21 I fill their treasuries.
8:22 “Yahweh possessed me in the beginning of his work,
8:22 before his deeds of old.
8:23 I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning,
8:23 before the earth existed.
8:24 When there were no depths, I was born,
8:24 when there were no springs abounding with water.
8:25 Before the mountains were settled in place,
8:25 before the hills, I was born;
8:26 while as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields,
8:26 nor the beginning of the dust of the world.
8:27 When he established the heavens, I was there.
8:27 When he set a circle on the surface of the deep,
8:28 when he established the clouds above,
8:28 when the springs of the deep became strong,
8:29 when he gave to the sea its boundary,
8:29 that the waters should not violate his commandment,
8:29 when he marked out the foundations of the earth,
8:30 then I was the craftsman by his side.
8:30 I was a delight day by day,
8:30 always rejoicing before him,
8:31 rejoicing in his whole world.
8:31 My delight was with the sons of men.
8:32 “Now therefore, my sons, listen to me,
8:32 for blessed are those who keep my ways.
8:33 Hear instruction, and be wise.
8:33 Don’t refuse it.
8:34 Blessed is the man who hears me,
8:34 watching daily at my gates,
8:34 waiting at my door posts.
8:35 For whoever finds me, finds life,
8:35 and will obtain favor from Yahweh.
8:36 But he who sins against me wrongs his own soul.
8:36 All those who hate me love death.”
9:1 Wisdom has built her house.
9:1 She has carved out her seven pillars.
9:2 She has prepared her meat.
9:2 She has mixed her wine.
9:2 She has also set her table.
9:3 She has sent out her maidens.
9:3 She cries from the highest places of the city:
9:4 “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!”
9:4 As for him who is void of understanding, she says to him,
9:5 “Come, eat some of my bread,
9:5 Drink some of the wine which I have mixed!
9:6 Leave your simple ways, and live.
9:6 Walk in the way of understanding.”
9:7 One who corrects a mocker invites insult.
9:7 One who reproves a wicked man invites abuse.
9:8 Don’t reprove a scoffer, lest he hate you.
9:8 Reprove a wise person, and he will love you.
9:9 Instruct a wise person, and he will be still wiser.
9:9 Teach a righteous person, and he will increase in learning.
9:10 The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of wisdom.
9:10 The knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.
9:11 For by me your days will be multiplied.
9:11 The years of your life will be increased.
9:12 If you are wise, you are wise for yourself.
9:12 If you mock, you alone will bear it.
9:13 The foolish woman is loud,
9:13 undisciplined, and knows nothing.
9:14 She sits at the door of her house,
9:14 on a seat in the high places of the city,
9:15 to call to those who pass by,
9:15 who go straight on their ways,
9:16 “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here.”
9:16 as for him who is void of understanding, she says to him,
9:17 “Stolen water is sweet.
9:17 Food eaten in secret is pleasant.”
9:18 But he doesn’t know that the departed spirits are there,
9:18 that her guests are in the depths of Sheol.
10:1 The proverbs of Solomon.
10:1 A wise son makes a glad father;
10:1 but a foolish son brings grief to his mother.
10:2 Treasures of wickedness profit nothing,
10:2 but righteousness delivers from death.
10:3 Yahweh will not allow the soul of the righteous to go hungry,
10:3 but he thrusts away the desire of the wicked.
10:4 He becomes poor who works with a lazy hand,
10:4 but the hand of the diligent brings wealth.
10:5 He who gathers in summer is a wise son,
10:5 but he who sleeps during the harvest is a son who causes shame.
10:6 Blessings are on the head of the righteous,
10:6 but violence covers the mouth of the wicked.
10:7 The memory of the righteous is blessed,
10:7 but the name of the wicked will rot.
10:8 The wise in heart accept commandments,
10:8 but a chattering fool will fall.
10:9 He who walks blamelessly walks surely,
10:9 but he who perverts his ways will be found out.
10:10 One winking with the eye causes sorrow,
10:10 but a chattering fool will fall.
10:11 The mouth of the righteous is a spring of life,
10:11 but violence covers the mouth of the wicked.
10:12 Hatred stirs up strife,
10:12 but love covers all wrongs.
10:13 Wisdom is found on the lips of him who has discernment,
10:13 but a rod is for the back of him who is void of understanding.
10:14 Wise men lay up knowledge,
10:14 but the mouth of the foolish is near ruin.
10:15 The rich man’s wealth is his strong city.
10:15 The destruction of the poor is their poverty.
10:16 The labor of the righteous leads to life.
10:16 The increase of the wicked leads to sin.
10:17 He is in the way of life who heeds correction,
10:17 but he who forsakes reproof leads others astray.
10:18 He who hides hatred has lying lips.
10:18 He who utters a slander is a fool.

28:10 Whoever causes the upright to go astray in an evil way,
28:10 he will fall into his own trap;
28:10 but the blameless will inherit good.
28:11 The rich man is wise in his own eyes;
28:11 but the poor who has understanding sees through him.
28:12 When the righteous triumph, there is great glory;
28:12 but when the wicked rise, men hide themselves.
28:13 He who conceals his sins doesn’t prosper,
28:13 but whoever confesses and renounces them finds mercy.
28:14 Blessed is the man who always fears;
28:14 but one who hardens his heart falls into trouble.
28:15 As a roaring lion or a charging bear,
28:15 so is a wicked ruler over helpless people.
28:16 A tyrannical ruler lacks judgment.
28:16 One who hates ill-gotten gain will have long days.
28:17 A man who is tormented by life blood will be a fugitive until death;
28:17 no one will support him.
28:18 Whoever walks blamelessly is kept safe;
28:18 but one with perverse ways will fall suddenly.
28:19 One who works his land will have an abundance of food;
28:19 but one who chases fantasies will have his fill of poverty.
28:20 A faithful man is rich with blessings;
28:20 but one who is eager to be rich will not go unpunished.
28:21 To show partiality is not good;
28:21 yet a man will do wrong for a piece of bread.
28:22 A stingy man hurries after riches,
28:22 and doesn’t know that poverty waits for him.
28:23 One who rebukes a man will afterward find more favor
28:23 than one who flatters with the tongue.
28:24 Whoever robs his father or his mother and says, “It’s not wrong,”
28:24 is a partner with a destroyer.
28:25 One who is greedy stirs up strife;
28:25 but one who trusts in Yahweh will prosper.
28:26 One who trusts in himself is a fool;
28:26 but one who walks in wisdom is kept safe.
28:27 One who gives to the poor has no lack;
28:27 but one who closes his eyes will have many curses.
28:28 When the wicked rise, men hide themselves;
28:28 but when they perish, the righteous thrive.
29:1 He who is often rebuked and stiffens his neck
29:1 will be destroyed suddenly, with no remedy.
29:2 When the righteous thrive, the people rejoice;
29:2 but when the wicked rule, the people groan.
29:3 Whoever loves wisdom brings joy to his father;
29:3 but a companion of prostitutes squanders his wealth.
29:4 The king by justice makes the land stable,
29:4 but he who takes bribes tears it down.
29:5 A man who flatters his neighbor
29:5 spreads a net for his feet.
29:6 An evil man is snared by his sin,
29:6 but the righteous can sing and be glad.
29:7 The righteous care about justice for the poor.
29:7 The wicked aren’t concerned about knowledge.
29:8 Mockers stir up a city,
29:8 but wise men turn away anger.
29:9 If a wise man goes to court with a foolish man,
29:9 the fool rages or scoffs, and there is no peace.
29:10 The bloodthirsty hate a man of integrity;
29:10 and they seek the life of the upright.
29:11 A fool vents all of his anger,
29:11 but a wise man brings himself under control.
29:12 If a ruler listens to lies,
29:12 all of his officials are wicked.
29:13 The poor man and the oppressor have this in common:
29:13 Yahweh gives sight to the eyes of both.
29:14 The king who fairly judges the poor,
29:14 his throne shall be established forever.
29:15 The rod of correction gives wisdom,
29:15 but a child left to himself causes shame to his mother.
29:16 When the wicked increase, sin increases;
29:16 but the righteous will see their downfall.
29:17 Correct your son, and he will give you peace;
29:17 yes, he will bring delight to your soul.
29:18 Where there is no revelation, the people cast off restraint;
29:18 but one who keeps the law is blessed.
29:19 A servant can’t be corrected by words.
29:19 Though he understands, yet he will not respond.
29:20 Do you see a man who is hasty in his words?
29:20 There is more hope for a fool than for him.
29:21 He who pampers his servant from youth
29:21 will have him become a son in the end.
29:22 An angry man stirs up strife,
29:22 and a wrathful man abounds in sin.
29:23 A man’s pride brings him low,
29:23 but one of lowly spirit gains honor.
29:24 Whoever is an accomplice of a thief is an enemy of his own soul.
29:24 He takes an oath, but dares not testify.
29:25 The fear of man proves to be a snare,
29:25 but whoever puts his trust in Yahweh is kept safe.
29:26 Many seek the ruler’s favor,
29:26 but a man’s justice comes from Yahweh.
29:27 A dishonest man detests the righteous,
29:27 and the upright in their ways detest the wicked.
30:1 The words of Agur the son of Jakeh; the revelation:
30:1 the man says to Ithiel,
30:1 to Ithiel and Ucal:
30:2 “Surely I am the most ignorant man,
30:2 and don’t have a man’s understanding.
30:3 I have not learned wisdom,
30:3 neither do I have the knowledge of the Holy One.
30:4 Who has ascended up into heaven, and descended?
30:4 Who has gathered the wind in his fists?
30:4 Who has bound the waters in his garment?
30:4 Who has established all the ends of the earth?
30:4 What is his name, and what is his son’s name, if you know?
30:5 “Every word of God is flawless.
30:5 He is a shield to those who take refuge in him.
30:6 Don’t you add to his words,
30:6 lest he reprove you, and you be found a liar.
30:7 “Two things I have asked of you.
30:7 Don’t deny me before I die.
30:8 Remove far from me falsehood and lies.
30:8 Give me neither poverty nor riches.
30:8 Feed me with the food that is needful for me,
30:9 lest I be full, deny you, and say, ‘Who is Yahweh?’
30:9 or lest I be poor, and steal,
30:9 and so dishonor the name of my God.
30:10 “Don’t slander a servant to his master,
30:10 lest he curse you, and you be held guilty.
30:11 There is a generation that curses their father,
30:11 and doesn’t bless their mother.
30:12 There is a generation that is pure in their own eyes,
30:12 yet are not washed from their filthiness.
30:13 There is a generation, oh how lofty are their eyes!
30:13 Their eyelids are lifted up.
30:14 There is a generation whose teeth are like swords,
30:14 and their jaws like knives,
30:14 to devour the poor from the earth, and the needy from among men.
30:15 “The leech has two daughters:
30:15 ‘Give, give.’
30:15 “There are three things that are never satisfied;
30:15 four that don’t say, ‘Enough:’
30:16 Sheol,
30:16 the barren womb;
30:16 the earth that is not satisfied with water;
30:16 and the fire that doesn’t say, ‘Enough.’
30:17 “The eye that mocks at his father,
30:17 and scorns obedience to his mother:
30:17 the ravens of the valley shall pick it out,
30:17 the young eagles shall eat it.
30:18 “There are three things which are too amazing for me,
30:18 four which I don’t understand:
30:19 The way of an eagle in the air,
30:19 the way of a serpent on a rock,
30:19 the way of a ship in the middle of the sea,
30:19 and the way of a man with a maiden.
30:20 “So is the way of an adulterous woman:
30:20 She eats and wipes her mouth,
30:20 and says, ‘I have done nothing wrong.’
30:21 “For three things the earth trembles,
30:21 and under four, it can’t bear up:
30:22 For a servant when he is king,
30:22 a fool when he is filled with food,
30:23 for an unloved woman when she is married,
30:23 and a servant who is heir to her mistress.
30:24 “There are four things which are little on the earth,
30:24 but they are exceedingly wise:
30:25 The ants are not a strong people,
30:25 yet they provide their food in the summer.
30:26 The hyraxes are but a feeble folk,
30:26 yet make they their houses in the rocks.
30:27 The locusts have no king,
30:27 yet they advance in ranks.
30:28 You can catch a lizard with your hands,
30:28 yet it is in kings’ palaces.
30:29 “There are three things which are stately in their march,
30:29 four which are stately in going:
30:30 The lion, which is mightiest among animals,
30:30 and doesn’t turn away for any;
30:31 the greyhound;
30:31 the male goat;
30:31 and the king against whom there is no rising up.
30:32 “If you have done foolishly in lifting up yourself,
30:32 or if you have thought evil,
30:32 put your hand over your mouth.
30:33 For as the churning of milk produces butter,
30:33 and the wringing of the nose produces blood;
30:33 so the forcing of wrath produces strife.”
31:1 The words of king Lemuel; the revelation which his mother taught him.
31:2 “Oh, my son!
31:2 Oh, son of my womb!
31:2 Oh, son of my vows!
31:3 Don’t give your strength to women,
31:3 nor your ways to that which destroys kings.
31:4 It is not for kings, Lemuel,
31:4 it is not for kings to drink wine,
31:4 nor for princes to say, ‘Where is strong drink?’
31:5 lest they drink, and forget the law,
31:5 and pervert the justice due to anyone who is afflicted.
31:6 Give strong drink to him who is ready to perish,
31:6 and wine to the bitter in soul.
31:7 Let him drink, and forget his poverty,
31:7 and remember his misery no more.
31:8 Open your mouth for the mute,
31:8 in the cause of all who are left desolate.
31:9 Open your mouth, judge righteously,
31:9 and serve justice to the poor and needy.”
31:10 Who can find a worthy woman?
31:10 For her price is far above rubies.
31:11 The heart of her husband trusts in her.
31:11 He shall have no lack of gain.
31:12 She does him good, and not harm,
31:12 all the days of her life.
31:13 She seeks wool and flax,
31:13 and works eagerly with her hands.
31:14 She is like the merchant ships.
31:14 She brings her bread from afar.
31:15 She rises also while it is yet night,
31:15 gives food to her household,
31:15 and portions for her servant girls.
31:16 She considers a field, and buys it.
31:16 With the fruit of her hands, she plants a vineyard.
31:17 She arms her waist with strength,
31:17 and makes her arms strong.
31:18 She perceives that her merchandise is profitable.
31:18 Her lamp doesn’t go out by night.
31:19 She lays her hands to the distaff,
31:19 and her hands hold the spindle.
31:20 She opens her arms to the poor;
31:20 yes, she extends her hands to the needy.
31:21 She is not afraid of the snow for her household;
31:21 for all her household are clothed with scarlet.
31:22 She makes for herself carpets of tapestry.
31:22 Her clothing is fine linen and purple.
31:23 Her husband is respected in the gates,
31:23 when he sits among the elders of the land.
31:24 She makes linen garments and sells them,
31:24 and delivers sashes to the merchant.
31:25 Strength and dignity are her clothing.
31:25 She laughs at the time to come.
31:26 She opens her mouth with wisdom.
31:26 Kind instruction is on her tongue.
31:27 She looks well to the ways of her household,
31:27 and doesn’t eat the bread of idleness.
31:28 Her children rise up and call her blessed.
31:28 Her husband also praises her:
31:29 “Many women do noble things,
31:29 but you excel them all.”
31:30 Charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain;
31:30 but a woman who fears Yahweh, she shall be praised.
31:31 Give her of the fruit of her hands!
31:31 Let her works praise her in the gates!
