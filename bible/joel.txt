1:1 Yahweh’s word that came to Joel, the son of Pethuel.
1:2 Hear this, you elders,
1:2 And listen, all you inhabitants of the land.
1:2 Has this ever happened in your days,
1:2 or in the days of your fathers?
1:3 Tell your children about it,
1:3 and have your children tell their children,
1:3 and their children, another generation.
1:4 What the swarming locust has left, the great locust has eaten.
1:4 What the great locust has left, the grasshopper has eaten.
1:4 What the grasshopper has left, the caterpillar has eaten.
1:5 Wake up, you drunkards, and weep!
1:5 Wail, all you drinkers of wine, because of the sweet wine;
1:5 for it is cut off from your mouth.
1:6 For a nation has come up on my land, strong, and without number.
1:6 His teeth are the teeth of a lion,
1:6 and he has the fangs of a lioness.
1:7 He has laid my vine waste,
1:7 and stripped my fig tree.
1:7 He has stripped its bark, and thrown it away.
1:7 Its branches are made white.
1:8 Mourn like a virgin dressed in sackcloth for the husband of her youth!
1:9 The meal offering and the drink offering are cut off from Yahweh’s house.
1:9 The priests, Yahweh’s ministers, mourn.
1:10 The field is laid waste.
1:10 The land mourns, for the grain is destroyed,
1:10 The new wine has dried up,
1:10 and the oil languishes.
1:11 Be confounded, you farmers!
1:11 Wail, you vineyard keepers;
1:11 for the wheat and for the barley;
1:11 for the harvest of the field has perished.
1:12 The vine has dried up, and the fig tree withered;
1:12 the pomegranate tree, the palm tree also, and the apple tree,
1:12 even all of the trees of the field are withered;
1:12 for joy has withered away from the sons of men.
1:13 Put on sackcloth and mourn, you priests!
1:13 Wail, you ministers of the altar.
1:13 Come, lie all night in sackcloth, you ministers of my God,
1:13 for the meal offering and the drink offering are withheld from your God’s house.
1:14 Sanctify a fast.
1:14 Call a solemn assembly.
1:14 Gather the elders,
1:14 and all the inhabitants of the land, to the house of Yahweh, your God,
1:14 and cry to Yahweh.
1:15 Alas for the day!
1:15 For the day of Yahweh is at hand,
1:15 and it will come as destruction from the Almighty.
1:16 Isn’t the food cut off before our eyes;
1:16 joy and gladness from the house of our God?
1:17 The seeds rot under their clods.
1:17 The granaries are laid desolate.
1:17 The barns are broken down, for the grain has withered.
1:18 How the animals groan!
1:18 The herds of livestock are perplexed, because they have no pasture.
1:18 Yes, the flocks of sheep are made desolate.
1:19 Yahweh, I cry to you,
1:19 For the fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness,
1:19 and the flame has burned all the trees of the field.
1:20 Yes, the animals of the field pant to you,
1:20 for the water brooks have dried up,
1:20 And the fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.
2:1 Blow the trumpet in Zion,
2:1 and sound an alarm in my holy mountain!
2:1 Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble,
2:1 for the day of Yahweh comes,
2:1 for it is close at hand:
2:2 A day of darkness and gloominess,
2:2 a day of clouds and thick darkness.
2:2 As the dawn spreading on the mountains,
2:2 a great and strong people;
2:2 there has never been the like,
2:2 neither will there be any more after them,
2:2 even to the years of many generations.
2:3 A fire devours before them,
2:3 and behind them, a flame burns.
2:3 The land is as the garden of Eden before them,
2:3 and behind them, a desolate wilderness.
2:3 Yes, and no one has escaped them.
2:4 Their appearance is as the appearance of horses,
2:4 and they run as horsemen.
2:5 Like the noise of chariots on the tops of the mountains, they leap,
2:5 like the noise of a flame of fire that devours the stubble,
2:5 like a strong people set in battle array.
2:6 At their presence the peoples are in anguish.
2:6 All faces have grown pale.
2:7 They run like mighty men.
2:7 They climb the wall like warriors.
2:7 They each march in his line, and they don’t swerve off course.
2:8 Neither does one jostle another;
2:8 they march everyone in his path,
2:8 and they burst through the defenses,
2:8 and don’t break ranks.
2:9 They rush on the city.
2:9 They run on the wall.
2:9 They climb up into the houses.
2:9 They enter in at the windows like thieves.
2:10 The earth quakes before them.
2:10 The heavens tremble.
2:10 The sun and the moon are darkened,
2:10 and the stars withdraw their shining.
2:11 Yahweh thunders his voice before his army;
2:11 for his forces are very great;
2:11 for he is strong who obeys his command;
2:11 for the day of Yahweh is great and very awesome,
2:11 and who can endure it?
2:12 “Yet even now,” says Yahweh, “turn to me with all your heart,
2:12 and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning.”
2:13 Tear your heart, and not your garments,
2:13 and turn to Yahweh, your God;
2:13 for he is gracious and merciful,
2:13 slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness,
2:13 and relents from sending calamity.
2:14 Who knows? He may turn and relent,
2:14 and leave a blessing behind him,
2:14 even a meal offering and a drink offering to Yahweh, your God.
2:15 Blow the trumpet in Zion!
2:15 Sanctify a fast.
2:15 Call a solemn assembly.
2:16 Gather the people.
2:16 Sanctify the assembly.
2:16 Assemble the elders.
2:16 Gather the children, and those who nurse from breasts.
2:16 Let the bridegroom go out of his room,
2:16 and the bride out of her room.
2:17 Let the priests, the ministers of Yahweh, weep between the porch and the altar,
2:17 and let them say, “Spare your people, Yahweh,
2:17 and don’t give your heritage to reproach,
2:17 that the nations should rule over them.
2:17 Why should they say among the peoples,
2:17 ‘Where is their God?’ ”
2:18 Then Yahweh was jealous for his land,
2:18 And had pity on his people.
2:19 Yahweh answered his people,
2:19 “Behold, I will send you grain, new wine, and oil,
2:19 and you will be satisfied with them;
2:19 and I will no more make you a reproach among the nations.
2:20 But I will remove the northern army far away from you,
2:20 and will drive it into a barren and desolate land,
2:20 its front into the eastern sea,
2:20 and its back into the western sea;
2:20 and its stench will come up,
2:20 and its bad smell will rise.”
2:20 Surely he has done great things.
2:21 Land, don’t be afraid.
2:21 Be glad and rejoice, for Yahweh has done great things.
2:22 Don’t be afraid, you animals of the field;
2:22 for the pastures of the wilderness spring up,
2:22 for the tree bears its fruit.
2:22 The fig tree and the vine yield their strength.
2:23 “Be glad then, you children of Zion,
2:23 and rejoice in Yahweh, your God;
2:23 for he gives you the early rain in just measure,
2:23 and he causes the rain to come down for you,
2:23 the early rain and the latter rain,
2:23 as before.
2:24 The threshing floors will be full of wheat,
2:24 and the vats will overflow with new wine and oil.
2:25 I will restore to you the years that the swarming locust has eaten,
2:25 the great locust, the grasshopper, and the caterpillar,
2:25 my great army, which I sent among you.
2:26 You will have plenty to eat, and be satisfied,
2:26 and will praise the name of Yahweh, your God,
2:26 who has dealt wondrously with you;
2:26 and my people will never again be disappointed.
2:27 You will know that I am among Israel,
2:27 and that I am Yahweh, your God, and there is no one else;
2:27 and my people will never again be disappointed.
2:28 “It will happen afterward, that I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh;
2:28 and your sons and your daughters will prophesy.
2:28 Your old men will dream dreams.
2:28 Your young men will see visions.
2:29 And also on the servants and on the handmaids in those days,
2:29 I will pour out my Spirit.
2:30 I will show wonders in the heavens and in the earth:
2:30 blood, fire, and pillars of smoke.
2:31 The sun will be turned into darkness,
2:31 and the moon into blood,
2:31 before the great and terrible day of Yahweh comes.
2:32 It will happen that whoever will call on Yahweh’s name shall be saved;
2:32 for in Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be those who escape,
2:32 as Yahweh has said,
2:32 and among the remnant, those whom Yahweh calls.
3:1 “For, behold, in those days,
3:1 and in that time,
3:1 when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,
3:2 I will gather all nations,
3:2 and will bring them down into the valley of Jehoshaphat;
3:2 and I will execute judgment on them there for my people,
3:2 and for my heritage, Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations.
3:2 They have divided my land,
3:3 and have cast lots for my people,
3:3 and have given a boy for a prostitute,
3:3 and sold a girl for wine, that they may drink.
3:4 “Yes, and what are you to me, Tyre, and Sidon,
3:4 and all the regions of Philistia?
3:4 Will you repay me?
3:4 And if you repay me,
3:4 I will swiftly and speedily return your repayment on your own head.
3:5 Because you have taken my silver and my gold,
3:5 and have carried my finest treasures into your temples,
3:6 and have sold the children of Judah and the children of Jerusalem to the sons of the Greeks,
3:6 that you may remove them far from their border.
3:7 Behold, I will stir them up out of the place where you have sold them,
3:7 and will return your repayment on your own head;
3:8 and I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hands of the children of Judah,
3:8 and they will sell them to the men of Sheba,
3:8 to a faraway nation,
3:8 for Yahweh has spoken it.”
3:9 Proclaim this among the nations:
3:9 “Prepare for war!
3:9 Stir up the mighty men.
3:9 Let all the warriors draw near.
3:9 Let them come up.
3:10 Beat your plowshares into swords,
3:10 and your pruning hooks into spears.
3:10 Let the weak say, ‘I am strong.’
3:11 Hurry and come, all you surrounding nations,
3:11 and gather yourselves together.”
3:11 Cause your mighty ones to come down there, Yahweh.
3:12 “Let the nations arouse themselves,
3:12 and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat;
3:12 for there I will sit to judge all the surrounding nations.
3:13 Put in the sickle;
3:13 for the harvest is ripe.
3:13 Come, tread, for the wine press is full,
3:13 the vats overflow, for their wickedness is great.”
3:14 Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision!
3:14 For the day of Yahweh is near, in the valley of decision.
3:15 The sun and the moon are darkened,
3:15 and the stars withdraw their shining.
3:16 Yahweh will roar from Zion,
3:16 and thunder from Jerusalem;
3:16 and the heavens and the earth will shake;
3:16 but Yahweh will be a refuge to his people,
3:16 and a stronghold to the children of Israel.
3:17 “So you will know that I am Yahweh, your God,
3:17 dwelling in Zion, my holy mountain.
3:17 Then Jerusalem will be holy,
3:17 and no strangers will pass through her any more.
3:18 It will happen in that day,
3:18 that the mountains will drop down sweet wine,
3:18 the hills will flow with milk,
3:18 all the brooks of Judah will flow with waters,
3:18 and a fountain will flow out from Yahweh’s house,
3:18 and will water the valley of Shittim.
3:19 Egypt will be a desolation,
3:19 and Edom will be a desolate wilderness,
3:19 for the violence done to the children of Judah,
3:19 because they have shed innocent blood in their land.
3:20 But Judah will be inhabited forever,
3:20 and Jerusalem from generation to generation.
3:21 I will cleanse their blood,
3:21 that I have not cleansed:
3:21 for Yahweh dwells in Zion.”
