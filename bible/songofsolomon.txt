1:1 The Song of songs, which is Solomon’s.
1:2 Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth;
1:2 for your love is better than wine.
1:3 Your oils have a pleasing fragrance.
1:3 Your name is oil poured out,
1:3 therefore the virgins love you.
1:4 Take me away with you.
1:4 Let’s hurry.
1:4 The king has brought me into his rooms.
1:4 We will be glad and rejoice in you.
1:4 We will praise your love more than wine!
1:4 They are right to love you.
1:5 I am dark, but lovely,
1:5 you daughters of Jerusalem,
1:5 like Kedar’s tents,
1:5 like Solomon’s curtains.
1:6 Don’t stare at me because I am dark,
1:6 because the sun has scorched me.
1:6 My mother’s sons were angry with me.
1:6 They made me keeper of the vineyards.
1:6 I haven’t kept my own vineyard.
1:7 Tell me, you whom my soul loves,
1:7 where you graze your flock,
1:7 where you rest them at noon;
1:7 for why should I be as one who is veiled
1:7 beside the flocks of your companions?
1:8 If you don’t know, most beautiful among women,
1:8 follow the tracks of the sheep.
1:8 Graze your young goats beside the shepherds’ tents.
1:9 I have compared you, my love,
1:9 to a steed in Pharaoh’s chariots.
1:10 Your cheeks are beautiful with earrings,
1:10 your neck with strings of jewels.
1:11 We will make you earrings of gold,
1:11 with studs of silver.
1:12 While the king sat at his table,
1:12 my perfume spread its fragrance.
1:13 My beloved is to me a sachet of myrrh,
1:13 that lies between my breasts.
1:14 My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms
1:14 from the vineyards of En Gedi.
1:15 Behold, you are beautiful, my love.
1:15 Behold, you are beautiful.
1:15 Your eyes are like doves.
1:16 Behold, you are beautiful, my beloved, yes, pleasant;
1:16 and our couch is verdant.
1:17 The beams of our house are cedars.
1:17 Our rafters are firs.
2:1 I am a rose of Sharon,
2:1 a lily of the valleys.
2:2 As a lily among thorns,
2:2 so is my love among the daughters.
2:3 As the apple tree among the trees of the wood,
2:3 so is my beloved among the sons.
2:3 I sat down under his shadow with great delight,
2:3 his fruit was sweet to my taste.
2:4 He brought me to the banquet hall.
2:4 His banner over me is love.
2:5 Strengthen me with raisins,
2:5 refresh me with apples;
2:5 for I am faint with love.
2:6 His left hand is under my head.
2:6 His right hand embraces me.
2:7 I adjure you, daughters of Jerusalem,
2:7 by the roes, or by the hinds of the field,
2:7 that you not stir up, nor awaken love,
2:7 until it so desires.
2:8 The voice of my beloved!
2:8 Behold, he comes,
2:8 leaping on the mountains,
2:8 skipping on the hills.
2:9 My beloved is like a roe or a young deer.
2:9 Behold, he stands behind our wall!
2:9 He looks in at the windows.
2:9 He glances through the lattice.
2:10 My beloved spoke, and said to me,
2:10 “Rise up, my love, my beautiful one, and come away.
2:11 For behold, the winter is past.
2:11 The rain is over and gone.
2:12 The flowers appear on the earth.
2:12 The time of the singing has come,
2:12 and the voice of the turtledove is heard in our land.
2:13 The fig tree ripens her green figs.
2:13 The vines are in blossom.
2:13 They give out their fragrance.
2:13 Arise, my love, my beautiful one,
2:13 and come away.”
2:14 My dove in the clefts of the rock,
2:14 in the hiding places of the mountainside,
2:14 let me see your face.
2:14 let me hear your voice;
2:14 for your voice is sweet and your face is lovely.
2:15 Catch for us the foxes,
2:15 the little foxes that plunder the vineyards;
2:15 for our vineyards are in blossom.
2:16 My beloved is mine, and I am his.
2:16 He browses among the lilies.
2:17 Until the day is cool, and the shadows flee away,
2:17 turn, my beloved,
2:17 and be like a roe or a young deer on the mountains of Bether.
3:1 By night on my bed,
3:1 I sought him whom my soul loves.
3:1 I sought him, but I didn’t find him.
3:2 I will get up now, and go about the city;
3:2 in the streets and in the squares I will seek him whom my soul loves.
3:2 I sought him, but I didn’t find him.
3:3 The watchmen who go about the city found me;
3:3 “Have you seen him whom my soul loves?”
3:4 I had scarcely passed from them,
3:4 when I found him whom my soul loves.
3:4 I held him, and would not let him go,
3:4 until I had brought him into my mother’s house,
3:4 into the room of her who conceived me.
3:5 I adjure you, daughters of Jerusalem,
3:5 by the roes, or by the hinds of the field,
3:5 that you not stir up nor awaken love,
3:5 until it so desires.
3:6 Who is this who comes up from the wilderness like pillars of smoke,
3:6 perfumed with myrrh and frankincense,
3:6 with all spices of the merchant?
3:7 Behold, it is Solomon’s carriage!
3:7 Sixty mighty men are around it,
3:7 of the mighty men of Israel.
3:8 They all handle the sword, and are expert in war.
3:8 Every man has his sword on his thigh,
3:8 because of fear in the night.
3:9 King Solomon made himself a carriage
3:9 of the wood of Lebanon.
3:10 He made its pillars of silver,
3:10 its bottom of gold, its seat of purple,
3:10 the middle of it being paved with love,
3:10 from the daughters of Jerusalem.
3:11 Go out, you daughters of Zion, and see king Solomon,
3:11 with the crown with which his mother has crowned him,
3:11 in the day of his weddings,
3:11 in the day of the gladness of his heart.
4:1 Behold, you are beautiful, my love.
4:1 Behold, you are beautiful.
4:1 Your eyes are like doves behind your veil.
4:1 Your hair is as a flock of goats,
4:1 that descend from Mount Gilead.
4:2 Your teeth are like a newly shorn flock,
4:2 which have come up from the washing,
4:2 where every one of them has twins.
4:2 None is bereaved among them.
4:3 Your lips are like scarlet thread.
4:3 Your mouth is lovely.
4:3 Your temples are like a piece of a pomegranate behind your veil.
4:4 Your neck is like David’s tower built for an armory,
4:4 on which a thousand shields hang,
4:4 all the shields of the mighty men.
4:5 Your two breasts are like two fawns
4:5 that are twins of a roe,
4:5 which feed among the lilies.
4:6 Until the day is cool, and the shadows flee away,
4:6 I will go to the mountain of myrrh,
4:6 to the hill of frankincense.
4:7 You are all beautiful, my love.
4:7 There is no spot in you.
4:8 Come with me from Lebanon, my bride,
4:8 with me from Lebanon.
4:8 Look from the top of Amana,
4:8 from the top of Senir and Hermon,
4:8 from the lions’ dens,
4:8 from the mountains of the leopards.
4:9 You have ravished my heart, my sister, my bride.
4:9 You have ravished my heart with one of your eyes,
4:9 with one chain of your neck.
4:10 How beautiful is your love, my sister, my bride!
4:10 How much better is your love than wine,
4:10 the fragrance of your perfumes than all kinds of spices!
4:11 Your lips, my bride, drip like the honeycomb.
4:11 Honey and milk are under your tongue.
4:11 The smell of your garments is like the smell of Lebanon.
4:12 My sister, my bride, is a locked up garden;
4:12 a locked up spring,
4:12 a sealed fountain.
4:13 Your shoots are an orchard of pomegranates, with precious fruits,
4:13 henna with spikenard plants,
4:14 spikenard and saffron,
4:14 calamus and cinnamon, with every kind of incense tree;
4:14 myrrh and aloes, with all the best spices,
4:15 a fountain of gardens,
4:15 a well of living waters,
4:15 flowing streams from Lebanon.
4:16 Awake, north wind, and come, you south!
4:16 Blow on my garden, that its spices may flow out.
4:16 Let my beloved come into his garden,
4:16 and taste his precious fruits.
5:1 I have come into my garden, my sister, my bride.
5:1 I have gathered my myrrh with my spice;
5:1 I have eaten my honeycomb with my honey;
5:1 I have drunk my wine with my milk.
5:1 Eat, friends!
5:1 Drink, yes, drink abundantly, beloved.
5:2 I was asleep, but my heart was awake.
5:2 It is the voice of my beloved who knocks:
5:2 “Open to me, my sister, my love, my dove, my undefiled;
5:2 for my head is filled with dew,
5:2 and my hair with the dampness of the night.”
5:3 I have taken off my robe. Indeed, must I put it on?
5:3 I have washed my feet. Indeed, must I soil them?
5:4 My beloved thrust his hand in through the latch opening.
5:4 My heart pounded for him.
5:5 I rose up to open for my beloved.
5:5 My hands dripped with myrrh,
5:5 my fingers with liquid myrrh,
5:5 on the handles of the lock.
5:6 I opened to my beloved;
5:6 but my beloved left, and had gone away.
5:6 My heart went out when he spoke.
5:6 I looked for him, but I didn’t find him.
5:6 I called him, but he didn’t answer.
5:7 The watchmen who go about the city found me.
5:7 They beat me.
5:7 They bruised me.
5:7 The keepers of the walls took my cloak away from me.
5:8 I adjure you, daughters of Jerusalem,
5:8 If you find my beloved,
5:8 that you tell him that I am faint with love.
5:9 How is your beloved better than another beloved,
5:9 you fairest among women?
5:9 How is your beloved better than another beloved,
5:9 that you do so adjure us?
5:10 My beloved is white and ruddy.
5:10 The best among ten thousand.
5:11 His head is like the purest gold.
5:11 His hair is bushy, black as a raven.
5:12 His eyes are like doves beside the water brooks,
5:12 washed with milk, mounted like jewels.
5:13 His cheeks are like a bed of spices with towers of perfumes.
5:13 His lips are like lilies, dropping liquid myrrh.
5:14 His hands are like rings of gold set with beryl.
5:14 His body is like ivory work overlaid with sapphires.
5:15 His legs are like pillars of marble set on sockets of fine gold.
5:15 His appearance is like Lebanon, excellent as the cedars.
5:16 His mouth is sweetness;
5:16 yes, he is altogether lovely.
5:16 This is my beloved, and this is my friend,
5:16 daughters of Jerusalem.

6:11 and the pomegranates were in flower.

6:12 my desire set me with my royal people’s chariots.
