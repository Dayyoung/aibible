1:1 Yahweh’s word that came to Micah the Morashtite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.
1:2 Hear, you peoples, all of you.
1:2 Listen, O earth, and all that is therein:
1:2 and let the Lord Yahweh be witness against you,
1:2 the Lord from his holy temple.
1:3 For, behold, Yahweh comes out of his place,
1:3 and will come down and tread on the high places of the earth.
1:4 The mountains melt under him,
1:4 and the valleys split apart,
1:4 like wax before the fire,
1:4 like waters that are poured down a steep place.
1:5 “All this is for the disobedience of Jacob,
1:5 and for the sins of the house of Israel.
1:5 What is the disobedience of Jacob?
1:5 Isn’t it Samaria?
1:5 And what are the high places of Judah?
1:5 Aren’t they Jerusalem?
1:6 Therefore I will make Samaria like a rubble heap of the field,
1:6 like places for planting vineyards;
1:6 and I will pour down its stones into the valley,
1:6 and I will uncover its foundations.
1:7 All her idols will be beaten to pieces,
1:7 and all her temple gifts will be burned with fire,
1:7 and all her images I will destroy;
1:7 for of the hire of a prostitute has she gathered them,
1:7 and to the hire of a prostitute shall they return.”
1:8 For this I will lament and wail;
1:8 I will go stripped and naked;
1:8 I will howl like the jackals,
1:8 and moan like the daughters of owls.
1:9 For her wounds are incurable;
1:9 for it has come even to Judah.
1:9 It reaches to the gate of my people,
1:9 even to Jerusalem.
1:10 Don’t tell it in Gath.
1:10 Don’t weep at all.
1:10 At Beth Ophrah I have rolled myself in the dust.
1:11 Pass on, inhabitant of Shaphir, in nakedness and shame.
1:11 The inhabitant of Zaanan won’t come out.
1:11 The wailing of Beth Ezel will take from you his protection.
1:12 For the inhabitant of Maroth waits anxiously for good,
1:12 because evil has come down from Yahweh to the gate of Jerusalem.
1:13 Harness the chariot to the swift steed, inhabitant of Lachish.
1:13 She was the beginning of sin to the daughter of Zion;
1:13 For the transgressions of Israel were found in you.
1:14 Therefore you will give a parting gift to Moresheth Gath.
1:14 The houses of Achzib will be a deceitful thing to the kings of Israel.
1:15 I will yet bring to you, inhabitant of Mareshah.
1:15 He who is the glory of Israel will come to Adullam.
1:16 Shave your heads,
1:16 and cut off your hair for the children of your delight.
1:16 Enlarge your baldness like the vulture;
1:16 for they have gone into captivity from you!
2:1 Woe to those who devise iniquity
2:1 and work evil on their beds!
2:1 When the morning is light, they practice it,
2:1 because it is in the power of their hand.
2:2 They covet fields, and seize them;
2:2 and houses, and take them away:
2:2 and they oppress a man and his house,
2:2 even a man and his heritage.
2:3 Therefore Yahweh says:
2:3 “Behold, I am planning against these people a disaster,
2:3 from which you will not remove your necks,
2:3 neither will you walk haughtily;
2:3 for it is an evil time.
2:4 In that day they will take up a parable against you,
2:4 and lament with a doleful lamentation, saying,
2:4 ‘We are utterly ruined!
2:4 My people’s possession is divided up.
2:4 Indeed he takes it from me and assigns our fields to traitors!’ ”
2:5 Therefore you will have no one who divides the land by lot in Yahweh’s assembly.
2:6 “Don’t prophesy!”
2:6 They prophesy.
2:6 “Don’t prophesy about these things.
2:6 Disgrace won’t overtake us.”
2:7 Shall it be said, O house of Jacob:
2:7 “Is Yahweh’s Spirit angry?
2:7 Are these his doings?
2:7 Don’t my words do good to him who walks blamelessly?”
2:8 But lately my people have risen up as an enemy.
2:8 You strip the robe and clothing from those who pass by without a care, returning from battle.
2:9 You drive the women of my people out from their pleasant houses;
2:9 from their young children you take away my blessing forever.
2:10 Arise, and depart!
2:10 For this is not your resting place,
2:10 because of uncleanness that destroys,
2:10 even with a grievous destruction.
2:11 If a man walking in a spirit of falsehood lies:
2:11 “I will prophesy to you of wine and of strong drink;”
2:11 he would be the prophet of this people.
2:12 I will surely assemble, Jacob, all of you;
2:12 I will surely gather the remnant of Israel;
2:12 I will put them together as the sheep of Bozrah,
2:12 as a flock in the middle of their pasture;
2:12 they will swarm with people.
2:13 He who breaks open the way goes up before them.
2:13 They break through the gate, and go out.
2:13 And their king passes on before them,
2:13 with Yahweh at their head.
3:1 I said,
3:1 “Please listen, you heads of Jacob,
3:1 and rulers of the house of Israel:
3:1 Isn’t it for you to know justice?
3:2 You who hate the good,
3:2 and love the evil;
3:2 who tear off their skin,
3:2 and their flesh from off their bones;
3:3 who also eat the flesh of my people,
3:3 and peel their skin from off them,
3:3 and break their bones,
3:3 and chop them in pieces, as for the pot,
3:3 and as meat within the cauldron.
3:4 Then they will cry to Yahweh,
3:4 but he will not answer them.
3:4 Yes, he will hide his face from them at that time,
3:4 because they made their deeds evil.”
3:5 Yahweh says concerning the prophets who lead my people astray; for those who feed their teeth, they proclaim, “Peace!” and whoever doesn’t provide for their mouths, they prepare war against him:
3:6 “Therefore night is over you, with no vision,
3:6 and it is dark to you, that you may not divine;
3:6 and the sun will go down on the prophets,
3:6 and the day will be black over them.
3:7 The seers shall be disappointed,
3:7 and the diviners confounded.
3:7 Yes, they shall all cover their lips;
3:7 for there is no answer from God.”
3:8 But as for me, I am full of power by Yahweh’s Spirit,
3:8 and of judgment, and of might,
3:8 to declare to Jacob his disobedience,
3:8 and to Israel his sin.
3:9 Please listen to this, you heads of the house of Jacob,
3:9 and rulers of the house of Israel,
3:9 who abhor justice,
3:9 and pervert all equity.
3:10 They build up Zion with blood,
3:10 and Jerusalem with iniquity.
3:11 Her leaders judge for bribes,
3:11 and her priests teach for a price,
3:11 and her prophets of it tell fortunes for money:
3:11 yet they lean on Yahweh, and say,
3:11 “Isn’t Yahweh among us?
3:11 No disaster will come on us.”
3:12 Therefore Zion for your sake will be plowed like a field,
3:12 and Jerusalem will become heaps of rubble,
3:12 and the mountain of the temple like the high places of a forest.
4:1 But in the latter days,
4:1 it will happen that the mountain of Yahweh’s temple will be established on the top of the mountains,
4:1 and it will be exalted above the hills;
4:1 and peoples will stream to it.
4:2 Many nations will go and say,
4:2 “Come! Let’s go up to the mountain of Yahweh,
4:2 and to the house of the God of Jacob;
4:2 and he will teach us of his ways,
4:2 and we will walk in his paths.”
4:2 For the law will go out of Zion,
4:2 and Yahweh’s word from Jerusalem;
4:3 and he will judge between many peoples,
4:3 and will decide concerning strong nations afar off.
4:3 They will beat their swords into plowshares,
4:3 and their spears into pruning hooks.
4:3 Nation will not lift up sword against nation,
4:3 neither will they learn war any more.
4:4 But they will sit every man under his vine and under his fig tree;
4:4 and no one will make them afraid:
4:4 For the mouth of Yahweh of Armies has spoken.
4:5 Indeed all the nations may walk in the name of their gods;
4:5 but we will walk in the name of Yahweh our God forever and ever.
4:6 “In that day,” says Yahweh,
4:6 “I will assemble that which is lame,
4:6 and I will gather that which is driven away,
4:6 and that which I have afflicted;
4:7 and I will make that which was lame a remnant,
4:7 and that which was cast far off a strong nation:
4:7 and Yahweh will reign over them on Mount Zion from then on, even forever.”
4:8 You, tower of the flock, the hill of the daughter of Zion,
4:8 to you it will come,
4:8 yes, the former dominion will come,
4:8 the kingdom of the daughter of Jerusalem.
4:9 Now why do you cry out aloud?
4:9 Is there no king in you?
4:9 Has your counselor perished,
4:9 that pains have taken hold of you as of a woman in travail?
4:10 Be in pain, and labor to give birth, daughter of Zion,
4:10 like a woman in travail;
4:10 for now you will go out of the city,
4:10 and will dwell in the field,
4:10 and will come even to Babylon.
4:10 There you will be rescued.
4:10 There Yahweh will redeem you from the hand of your enemies.
4:11 Now many nations have assembled against you, that say,
4:11 “Let her be defiled,
4:11 and let our eye gloat over Zion.”
4:12 But they don’t know the thoughts of Yahweh,
4:12 neither do they understand his counsel;
4:12 for he has gathered them like the sheaves to the threshing floor.
4:13 Arise and thresh, daughter of Zion;
4:13 for I will make your horn iron,
4:13 and I will make your hoofs bronze;
4:13 and you will beat in pieces many peoples:
4:13 and I will devote their gain to Yahweh,
4:13 and their substance to the Lord of the whole earth.
5:1 Now you shall gather yourself in troops,
5:1 daughter of troops.
5:1 He has laid siege against us.
5:1 They will strike the judge of Israel with a rod on the cheek.
5:2 But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah,
5:2 being small among the clans of Judah,
5:2 out of you one will come out to me that is to be ruler in Israel;
5:2 whose goings out are from of old, from ancient times.
5:3 Therefore he will abandon them until the time that she who is in labor gives birth.
5:3 Then the rest of his brothers will return to the children of Israel.
5:4 He shall stand, and shall shepherd in the strength of Yahweh,
5:4 in the majesty of the name of Yahweh his God:
5:4 and they will live, for then he will be great to the ends of the earth.
5:5 He will be our peace when Assyria invades our land,
5:5 and when he marches through our fortresses,
5:5 then we will raise against him seven shepherds,
5:5 and eight leaders of men.
5:6 They will rule the land of Assyria with the sword,
5:6 and the land of Nimrod in its gates.
5:6 He will deliver us from the Assyrian,
5:6 when he invades our land,
5:6 and when he marches within our border.
5:7 The remnant of Jacob will be among many peoples,
5:7 like dew from Yahweh,
5:7 like showers on the grass,
5:7 that don’t wait for man,
5:7 nor wait for the sons of men.
5:8 The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations,
5:8 among many peoples,
5:8 like a lion among the animals of the forest,
5:8 like a young lion among the flocks of sheep;
5:8 who, if he goes through, treads down and tears in pieces,
5:8 and there is no one to deliver.
5:9 Let your hand be lifted up above your adversaries,
5:9 and let all of your enemies be cut off.
5:10 “It will happen in that day”, says Yahweh,
5:10 “that I will cut off your horses out from among you,
5:10 and will destroy your chariots.
5:11 I will cut off the cities of your land,
5:11 and will tear down all your strongholds.
5:12 I will destroy witchcraft from your hand;
5:12 and you shall have no soothsayers.
5:13 I will cut off your engraved images and your pillars out from among you;
5:13 and you shall no more worship the work of your hands.
5:14 I will uproot your Asherah poles out from among you;
5:14 and I will destroy your cities.
5:15 I will execute vengeance in anger,
5:15 and wrath on the nations that didn’t listen.”
6:1 Listen now to what Yahweh says:
6:1 “Arise, plead your case before the mountains,
6:1 and let the hills hear what you have to say.
6:2 Hear, you mountains, Yahweh’s controversy,
6:2 and you enduring foundations of the earth;
6:2 for Yahweh has a controversy with his people,
6:2 and he will contend with Israel.
6:3 My people, what have I done to you?
6:3 How have I burdened you?
6:3 Answer me!
6:4 For I brought you up out of the land of Egypt,
6:4 and redeemed you out of the house of bondage.
6:4 I sent before you Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.
6:5 My people, remember now what Balak king of Moab devised,
6:5 and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him from Shittim to Gilgal,
6:5 that you may know the righteous acts of Yahweh.”
6:6 How shall I come before Yahweh,
6:6 and bow myself before the exalted God?
6:6 Shall I come before him with burnt offerings,
6:6 with calves a year old?
6:7 Will Yahweh be pleased with thousands of rams?
6:7 With tens of thousands of rivers of oil?
6:7 Shall I give my firstborn for my disobedience?
6:7 The fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?
6:8 He has shown you, O man, what is good.
6:8 What does Yahweh require of you, but to act justly,
6:8 to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?
6:9 Yahweh’s voice calls to the city,
6:9 and wisdom sees your name:
6:9 “Listen to the rod,
6:9 and he who appointed it.
6:10 Are there yet treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked,
6:10 and a short ephah that is accursed?
6:11 Shall I be pure with dishonest scales,
6:11 and with a bag of deceitful weights?
6:12 Her rich men are full of violence,
6:12 her inhabitants speak lies,
6:12 and their tongue is deceitful in their speech.
6:13 Therefore I also have struck you with a grievous wound.
6:13 I have made you desolate because of your sins.
6:14 You shall eat, but not be satisfied.
6:14 Your humiliation will be within you.
6:14 You will store up, but not save;
6:14 and that which you save I will give up to the sword.
6:15 You will sow, but won’t reap.
6:15 You will tread the olives, but won’t anoint yourself with oil;
6:15 and crush grapes, but won’t drink the wine.
6:16 For the statutes of Omri are kept,
6:16 and all the works of Ahab’s house.
6:16 You walk in their counsels,
6:16 that I may make you a ruin,
6:16 and her inhabitants a hissing;
6:16 And you will bear the reproach of my people.”
7:1 Misery is mine!
7:1 Indeed, I am like one who gathers the summer fruits, as gleanings of the vineyard:
7:1 There is no cluster of grapes to eat.
7:1 My soul desires to eat the early fig.
7:2 The godly man has perished out of the earth,
7:2 and there is no one upright among men.
7:2 They all lie in wait for blood;
7:2 every man hunts his brother with a net.
7:3 Their hands are on that which is evil to do it diligently.
7:3 The ruler and judge ask for a bribe;
7:3 and the powerful man dictates the evil desire of his soul.
7:3 Thus they conspire together.
7:4 The best of them is like a brier.
7:4 The most upright is worse than a thorn hedge.
7:4 The day of your watchmen,
7:4 even your visitation, has come;
7:4 now is the time of their confusion.
7:5 Don’t trust in a neighbor.
7:5 Don’t put confidence in a friend.
7:5 With the woman lying in your embrace,
7:5 be careful of the words of your mouth!
7:6 For the son dishonors the father,
7:6 the daughter rises up against her mother,
7:6 the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;
7:6 a man’s enemies are the men of his own house.
7:7 But as for me, I will look to Yahweh.
7:7 I will wait for the God of my salvation.
7:7 My God will hear me.
7:8 Don’t rejoice against me, my enemy.
7:8 When I fall, I will arise.
7:8 When I sit in darkness, Yahweh will be a light to me.
7:9 I will bear the indignation of Yahweh,
7:9 because I have sinned against him,
7:9 until he pleads my case, and executes judgment for me.
7:9 He will bring me out to the light.
7:9 I will see his righteousness.
7:10 Then my enemy will see it,
7:10 and shame will cover her who said to me,
7:10 where is Yahweh your God?
7:10 Then my enemy will see me and will cover her shame.
7:10 Now she will be trodden down like the mire of the streets.
7:11 A day to build your walls—
7:11 In that day, he will extend your boundary.
7:12 In that day they will come to you from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,
7:12 and from Egypt even to the River,
7:12 and from sea to sea,
7:12 and mountain to mountain.
7:13 Yet the land will be desolate because of those who dwell therein,
7:13 for the fruit of their doings.
7:14 Shepherd your people with your staff,
7:14 the flock of your heritage,
7:14 who dwell by themselves in a forest,
7:14 in the middle of fertile pasture land, let them feed;
7:14 in Bashan and Gilead, as in the days of old.
7:15 “As in the days of your coming out of the land of Egypt,
7:15 I will show them marvelous things.”
7:16 The nations will see and be ashamed of all their might.
7:16 They will lay their hand on their mouth.
7:16 Their ears will be deaf.
7:17 They will lick the dust like a serpent.
7:17 Like crawling things of the earth they shall come trembling out of their dens.
7:17 They will come with fear to Yahweh our God,
7:17 and will be afraid because of you.
7:18 Who is a God like you, who pardons iniquity,
7:18 and passes over the disobedience of the remnant of his heritage?
7:18 He doesn’t retain his anger forever,
7:18 because he delights in loving kindness.
7:19 He will again have compassion on us.
7:19 He will tread our iniquities under foot;
7:19 and you will cast all their sins into the depths of the sea.
7:20 You will give truth to Jacob,
7:20 and mercy to Abraham,
7:20 as you have sworn to our fathers from the days of old.
