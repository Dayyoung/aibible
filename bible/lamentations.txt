1:1 How the city sits solitary,
1:1 that was full of people!
1:1 She has become as a widow,
1:1 who was great among the nations!
1:1 She who was a princess among the provinces
1:1 has become a slave!
1:2 She weeps bitterly in the night.
1:2 Her tears are on her cheeks.
1:2 Among all her lovers
1:2 she has no one to comfort her.
1:2 All her friends have dealt treacherously with her.
1:2 They have become her enemies.
1:3 Judah has gone into captivity because of affliction,
1:3 and because of great servitude.
1:3 She dwells among the nations.
1:3 She finds no rest.
1:3 All her persecutors overtook her within the straits.
1:4 The roads to Zion mourn,
1:4 because no one comes to the solemn assembly.
1:4 All her gates are desolate.
1:4 Her priests sigh.
1:4 Her virgins are afflicted,
1:4 and she herself is in bitterness.
1:5 Her adversaries have become the head.
1:5 Her enemies prosper;
1:5 for Yahweh has afflicted her for the multitude of her transgressions.
1:5 Her young children have gone into captivity before the adversary.
1:6 All majesty has departed from the daughter of Zion.
1:6 Her princes have become like deer that find no pasture.
1:6 They have gone without strength before the pursuer.
1:7 Jerusalem remembers in the days of her affliction and of her miseries
1:7 all her pleasant things that were from the days of old;
1:7 when her people fell into the hand of the adversary,
1:7 and no one helped her.
1:7 The adversaries saw her.
1:7 They mocked at her desolations.
1:8 Jerusalem has grievously sinned.
1:8 Therefore she has become unclean.
1:8 All who honored her despise her,
1:8 because they have seen her nakedness.
1:8 Yes, she sighs, and turns backward.
1:9 Her filthiness was in her skirts.
1:9 She didn’t remember her latter end.
1:9 Therefore she has come down astoundingly.
1:9 She has no comforter.
1:9 “See, Yahweh, my affliction;
1:9 for the enemy has magnified himself.”
1:10 The adversary has spread out his hand on all her pleasant things;
1:10 for she has seen that the nations have entered into her sanctuary,
1:10 concerning whom you commanded that they should not enter into your assembly.
1:11 All her people sigh.
1:11 They seek bread.
1:11 They have given their pleasant things for food to refresh their soul.
1:11 “Look, Yahweh, and see;
1:11 for I have become despised.”
1:12 “Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by?
1:12 Look, and see if there is any sorrow like my sorrow,
1:12 which is brought on me,
1:12 with which Yahweh has afflicted me in the day of his fierce anger.
1:13 “From on high has he sent fire into my bones,
1:13 and it prevails against them.
1:13 He has spread a net for my feet.
1:13 He has turned me back.
1:13 He has made me desolate and I faint all day long.
1:14 “The yoke of my transgressions is bound by his hand.
1:14 They are knit together.
1:14 They have come up on my neck.
1:14 He made my strength fail.
1:14 The Lord has delivered me into their hands,
1:14 against whom I am not able to stand.
1:15 “The Lord has set at nothing all my mighty men within me.
1:15 He has called a solemn assembly against me to crush my young men.
1:15 The Lord has trodden the virgin daughter of Judah as in a wine press.
1:16 “For these things I weep.
1:16 My eye, my eye runs down with water,
1:16 because the comforter who should refresh my soul is far from me.
1:16 My children are desolate,
1:16 because the enemy has prevailed.”
1:17 Zion spreads out her hands.
1:17 There is no one to comfort her.
1:17 Yahweh has commanded concerning Jacob,
1:17 that those who are around him should be his adversaries.
1:17 Jerusalem is among them as an unclean thing.
1:18 “Yahweh is righteous;
1:18 for I have rebelled against his commandment.
1:18 Please hear all you peoples,
1:18 and see my sorrow.
1:18 My virgins and my young men have gone into captivity.
1:19 “I called for my lovers,
1:19 but they deceived me.
1:19 My priests and my elders gave up the spirit in the city,
1:19 while they sought food for themselves to refresh their souls.
1:20 “Look, Yahweh; for I am in distress.
1:20 My heart is troubled.
1:20 My heart turns over within me,
1:20 for I have grievously rebelled.
1:20 Abroad, the sword bereaves.
1:20 At home, it is like death.
1:21 “They have heard that I sigh.
1:21 There is no one to comfort me.
1:21 All my enemies have heard of my trouble.
1:21 They are glad that you have done it.
1:21 You will bring the day that you have proclaimed,
1:21 and they will be like me.
1:22 “Let all their wickedness come before you.
1:22 Do to them as you have done to me for all my transgressions.
1:22 For my sighs are many,
1:22 and my heart is faint.
2:1 How has the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger!
2:1 He has cast the beauty of Israel down from heaven to the earth,
2:1 and hasn’t remembered his footstool in the day of his anger.
2:2 The Lord has swallowed up all the dwellings of Jacob
2:2 without pity.
2:2 He has thrown down in his wrath the strongholds of the daughter of Judah.
2:2 He has brought them down to the ground.
2:2 He has profaned the kingdom and its princes.
2:3 He has cut off all the horn of Israel in fierce anger.
2:3 He has drawn back his right hand from before the enemy.
2:3 He has burned up Jacob like a flaming fire,
2:3 which devours all around.
2:4 He has bent his bow like an enemy.
2:4 He has stood with his right hand as an adversary.
2:4 Has killed all that were pleasant to the eye.
2:4 In the tent of the daughter of Zion, he has poured out his wrath like fire.
2:5 The Lord has become as an enemy.
2:5 He has swallowed up Israel.
2:5 He has swallowed up all her palaces.
2:5 He has destroyed his strongholds.
2:5 He has multiplied mourning and lamentation in the daughter of Judah.
2:6 He has violently taken away his tabernacle,
2:6 as if it were of a garden.
2:6 He has destroyed his place of assembly.
2:6 Yahweh has caused solemn assembly and Sabbath to be forgotten in Zion.
2:6 In the indignation of his anger, he has despised the king and the priest.
2:7 The Lord has cast off his altar.
2:7 He has abhorred his sanctuary.
2:7 He has given the walls of her palaces into the hand of the enemy.
2:7 They have made a noise in Yahweh’s house,
2:7 as in the day of a solemn assembly.
2:8 Yahweh has purposed to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion.
2:8 He has stretched out the line.
2:8 He has not withdrawn his hand from destroying;
2:8 He has made the rampart and wall lament.
2:8 They languish together.
2:9 Her gates have sunk into the ground.
2:9 He has destroyed and broken her bars.
2:9 Her king and her princes are among the nations where the law is not.
2:9 Yes, her prophets find no vision from Yahweh.
2:10 The elders of the daughter of Zion sit on the ground.
2:10 They keep silence.
2:10 They have cast up dust on their heads.
2:10 They have clothed themselves with sackcloth.
2:10 The virgins of Jerusalem hang down their heads to the ground.
2:11 My eyes fail with tears.
2:11 My heart is troubled.
2:11 My liver is poured on the earth,
2:11 because of the destruction of the daughter of my people,
2:11 because the young children and the infants swoon in the streets of the city.
2:12 They ask their mothers,
2:12 “Where is grain and wine?”
2:12 when they swoon as the wounded in the streets of the city,
2:12 when their soul is poured out into their mothers’ bosom.
2:13 What shall I testify to you?
2:13 What shall I liken to you, daughter of Jerusalem?
2:13 What shall I compare to you,
2:13 that I may comfort you, virgin daughter of Zion?
2:13 For your breach is as big as the sea.
2:13 Who can heal you?
2:14 Your prophets have seen false and foolish visions for you.
2:14 They have not uncovered your iniquity,
2:14 to reverse your captivity,
2:14 but have seen for you false revelations and causes of banishment.
2:15 All that pass by clap their hands at you.
2:15 They hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying,
2:15 “Is this the city that men called ‘The perfection of beauty,
2:15 the joy of the whole earth’?”
2:16 All your enemies have opened their mouth wide against you.
2:16 They hiss and gnash their teeth.
2:16 They say, “We have swallowed her up.
2:16 Certainly this is the day that we looked for.
2:16 We have found it.
2:16 We have seen it.”
2:17 Yahweh has done that which he planned.
2:17 He has fulfilled his word that he commanded in the days of old.
2:17 He has thrown down,
2:17 and has not pitied.
2:17 He has caused the enemy to rejoice over you.
2:17 He has exalted the horn of your adversaries.
2:18 Their heart cried to the Lord.
2:18 O wall of the daughter of Zion,
2:18 let tears run down like a river day and night.
2:18 Give yourself no relief.
2:18 Don’t let the your eyes rest.
2:19 Arise, cry out in the night,
2:19 at the beginning of the watches!
2:19 Pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord.
2:19 Lift up your hands toward him for the life of your young children,
2:19 who faint for hunger at the head of every street.
2:20 “Look, Yahweh, and see to whom you have done thus!
2:20 Should the women eat their offspring,
2:20 the children that they held and bounced on their knees?
2:20 Should the priest and the prophet be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?
2:21 “The youth and the old man lie on the ground in the streets.
2:21 My virgins and my young men have fallen by the sword.
2:21 You have killed them in the day of your anger.
2:21 You have slaughtered, and not pitied.
2:22 “You have called, as in the day of a solemn assembly, my terrors on every side.
2:22 There was no one that escaped or remained in the day of Yahweh’s anger.
2:22 My enemy has consumed those whom I have cared for and brought up.
3:1 I am the man who has seen affliction
3:1 by the rod of his wrath.
3:2 He has led me and caused me to walk in darkness,
3:2 and not in light.
3:3 Surely he turns his hand against me
3:3 again and again all day long.
3:4 He has made my flesh and my skin old.
3:4 He has broken my bones.
3:5 He has built against me,
3:5 and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship.
3:6 He has made me dwell in dark places,
3:6 as those who have been long dead.
3:7 He has walled me about, so that I can’t go out.
3:7 He has made my chain heavy.
3:8 Yes, when I cry, and call for help,
3:8 he shuts out my prayer.
3:9 He has walled up my ways with cut stone.
3:9 He has made my paths crooked.
3:10 He is to me as a bear lying in wait,
3:10 as a lion in secret places.
3:11 He has turned away my ways,
3:11 and pulled me in pieces.
3:11 He has made me desolate.
3:12 He has bent his bow,
3:12 and set me as a mark for the arrow.
3:13 He has caused the shafts of his quiver to enter into my kidneys.
3:14 I have become a derision to all my people,
3:14 and their song all day long.
3:15 He has filled me with bitterness.
3:15 He has stuffed me with wormwood.
3:16 He has also broken my teeth with gravel.
3:16 He has covered me with ashes.
3:17 You have removed my soul far away from peace.
3:17 I forgot prosperity.
3:18 I said, “My strength has perished,
3:18 along with my expectation from Yahweh.”
3:19 Remember my affliction and my misery,
3:19 the wormwood and the bitterness.
3:20 My soul still remembers them,
3:20 and is bowed down within me.
3:21 This I recall to my mind;
3:21 therefore I have hope.
3:22 It is because of Yahweh’s loving kindnesses that we are not consumed,
3:22 because his compassion doesn’t fail.
3:23 They are new every morning.
3:23 Great is your faithfulness.
3:24 “Yahweh is my portion,” says my soul.
3:24 “Therefore I will hope in him.”
3:25 Yahweh is good to those who wait for him,
3:25 to the soul who seeks him.
3:26 It is good that a man should hope
3:26 and quietly wait for the salvation of Yahweh.
3:27 It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.
3:28 Let him sit alone and keep silence,
3:28 because he has laid it on him.
3:29 Let him put his mouth in the dust,
3:29 if it is so that there may be hope.
3:30 Let him give his cheek to him who strikes him.
3:30 Let him be filled full of reproach.
3:31 For the Lord will not cast off forever.
3:32 For though he causes grief,
3:32 yet he will have compassion according to the multitude of his loving kindnesses.
3:33 For he does not afflict willingly,
3:33 nor grieve the children of men.
3:34 To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,
3:35 to turn away the right of a man before the face of the Most High,
3:36 to subvert a man in his cause, the Lord doesn’t approve.
3:37 Who is he who says, and it comes to pass,
3:37 when the Lord doesn’t command it?
3:38 Doesn’t evil and good come out of the mouth of the Most High?
3:39 Why does a living man complain,
3:39 a man for the punishment of his sins?
3:40 Let us search and try our ways,
3:40 and turn again to Yahweh.
3:41 Let’s lift up our heart with our hands to God in the heavens.
3:42 “We have transgressed and have rebelled.
3:42 You have not pardoned.
3:43 “You have covered us with anger and pursued us.
3:43 You have killed.
3:43 You have not pitied.
3:44 You have covered yourself with a cloud,
3:44 so that no prayer can pass through.
3:45 You have made us an off-scouring and refuse
3:45 in the middle of the peoples.
3:46 “All our enemies have opened their mouth wide against us.
3:47 Terror and the pit have come on us,
3:47 devastation and destruction.”
3:48 My eye runs down with streams of water,
3:48 for the destruction of the daughter of my people.
3:49 My eye pours down
3:49 and doesn’t cease,
3:49 without any intermission,
3:50 until Yahweh looks down,
3:50 and sees from heaven.
3:51 My eye affects my soul,
3:51 because of all the daughters of my city.
3:52 They have chased me relentlessly like a bird,
3:52 those who are my enemies without cause.
3:53 They have cut off my life in the dungeon,
3:53 and have cast a stone on me.
3:54 Waters flowed over my head.
3:54 I said, “I am cut off.”
3:55 I called on your name, Yahweh,
3:55 out of the lowest dungeon.
3:56 You heard my voice:
3:56 “Don’t hide your ear from my sighing,
3:56 and my cry.”
3:57 You came near in the day that I called on you.
3:57 You said, “Don’t be afraid.”
3:58 Lord, you have pleaded the causes of my soul.
3:58 You have redeemed my life.
3:59 Yahweh, you have seen my wrong.
3:59 Judge my cause.
3:60 You have seen all their vengeance
3:60 and all their plans against me.
3:61 You have heard their reproach, Yahweh,
3:61 and all their plans against me,
3:62 the lips of those that rose up against me,
3:62 and their plots against me all day long.
3:63 You see their sitting down and their rising up.
3:63 I am their song.
3:64 You will pay them back, Yahweh,
3:64 according to the work of their hands.
3:65 You will give them hardness of heart,
3:65 your curse to them.
3:66 You will pursue them in anger,
3:66 and destroy them from under the heavens of Yahweh.
4:1 How the gold has become dim!
4:1 The most pure gold has changed!
4:1 The stones of the sanctuary are poured out
4:1 at the head of every street.
4:2 The precious sons of Zion,
4:2 comparable to fine gold,
4:2 how they are esteemed as earthen pitchers,
4:2 the work of the hands of the potter!
4:3 Even the jackals offer their breast.
4:3 They nurse their young ones.
4:3 But the daughter of my people has become cruel,
4:3 like the ostriches in the wilderness.
4:4 The tongue of the nursing child clings to the roof of his mouth for thirst.
4:4 The young children ask bread,
4:4 and no one breaks it for them.
4:5 Those who ate delicacies are desolate in the streets.
4:5 Those who were brought up in purple embrace dunghills.
4:6 For the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater than the sin of Sodom,
4:6 which was overthrown as in a moment.
4:6 No hands were laid on her.
4:7 Her nobles were purer than snow.
4:7 They were whiter than milk.
4:7 They were more ruddy in body than rubies.
4:7 Their polishing was like sapphire.
4:8 Their appearance is blacker than a coal.
4:8 They are not known in the streets.
4:8 Their skin clings to their bones.
4:8 It is withered.
4:8 It has become like a stick.
4:9 Those who are killed with the sword are better than those who are killed with hunger;
4:9 For these pine away, stricken through,
4:9 for lack of the fruits of the field.
4:10 The hands of the pitiful women have boiled their own children.
4:10 They were their food in the destruction of the daughter of my people.
4:11 Yahweh has accomplished his wrath.
4:11 He has poured out his fierce anger.
4:11 He has kindled a fire in Zion,
4:11 which has devoured its foundations.
4:12 The kings of the earth didn’t believe,
4:12 neither did all the inhabitants of the world,
4:12 that the adversary and the enemy would enter into the gates of Jerusalem.
4:13 It is because of the sins of her prophets
4:13 and the iniquities of her priests,
4:13 That have shed the blood of the just in the middle of her.
4:14 They wander as blind men in the streets.
4:14 They are polluted with blood,
4:14 So that men can’t touch their garments.
4:15 “Go away!” they cried to them.
4:15 “Unclean! Go away! Go away! Don’t touch!
4:15 When they fled away and wandered, men said among the nations,
4:15 “They can’t live here any more.”
4:16 Yahweh’s anger has scattered them.
4:16 He will not pay attention to them any more.
4:16 They didn’t respect the persons of the priests.
4:16 They didn’t favor the elders.
4:17 Our eyes still fail,
4:17 looking in vain for our help.
4:17 In our watching we have watched for a nation that could not save.
4:18 They hunt our steps,
4:18 so that we can’t go in our streets.
4:18 Our end is near.
4:18 Our days are fulfilled,
4:18 for our end has come.
4:19 Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of the sky.
4:19 They chased us on the mountains.
4:19 They set an ambush for us in the wilderness.
4:20 The breath of our nostrils,
4:20 the anointed of Yahweh,
4:20 was taken in their pits;
4:20 of whom we said,
4:20 under his shadow we will live among the nations.
4:21 Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Edom,
4:21 that dwells in the land of Uz.
4:21 The cup will pass through to you also.
4:21 You will be drunken,
4:21 and will make yourself naked.
4:22 The punishment of your iniquity is accomplished, daughter of Zion.
4:22 He will no more carry you away into captivity.
4:22 He will visit your iniquity, daughter of Edom.
4:22 He will uncover your sins.
5:1 Remember, Yahweh, what has come on us.
5:1 Look, and see our reproach.
5:2 Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,
5:2 our houses to aliens.
5:3 We are orphans and fatherless.
5:3 Our mothers are as widows.
5:4 We have drunken our water for money.
5:4 Our wood is sold to us.
5:5 Our pursuers are on our necks.
5:5 We are weary, and have no rest.
5:6 We have given our hands to the Egyptians,
5:6 and to the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.
5:7 Our fathers sinned, and are no more.
5:7 We have borne their iniquities.
5:8 Servants rule over us.
5:8 There is no one to deliver us out of their hand.
5:9 We get our bread at the peril of our lives,
5:9 because of the sword of the wilderness.
5:10 Our skin is black like an oven,
5:10 because of the burning heat of famine.
5:11 They ravished the women in Zion,
5:11 the virgins in the cities of Judah.
5:12 Princes were hanged up by their hands.
5:12 The faces of elders were not honored.
5:13 The young men carry millstones.
5:13 The children stumbled under loads of wood.
5:14 The elders have ceased from the gate,
5:14 and the young men from their music.
5:15 The joy of our heart has ceased.
5:15 Our dance is turned into mourning.
5:16 The crown has fallen from our head.
5:16 Woe to us, for we have sinned!
5:17 For this our heart is faint.
5:17 For these things our eyes are dim.
5:18 For the mountain of Zion, which is desolate.
5:18 The foxes walk on it.
5:19 You, Yahweh, remain forever.
5:19 Your throne is from generation to generation.
5:20 Why do you forget us forever,
5:20 and forsake us for so long a time?
5:21 Turn us to yourself, Yahweh, and we will be turned.
5:21 Renew our days as of old.
5:22 But you have utterly rejected us.
5:22 You are very angry against us.
